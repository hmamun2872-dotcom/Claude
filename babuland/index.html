Questions

    
Good to know

  

  
What are the opening hours?
Friday, Saturday and government holidays: 11 AM to 10 PM. Sunday to Thursday: 3 PM to 10 PM.

  
Do we need socks?
Yes, socks are mandatory for all play zones. Bring your own and your baby's, or buy a pair at the counter for 60 tk + VAT. They are reusable on future visits.

  
Can we bring our own food?
Please do not bring outside food to Babuland. Delicious food options are available inside.

  
Is there a height limit?
The Child ticket is for children up to 4.3 ft (130 cm).

  
What about babies?
Infants aged 6 to 11 months can use the soft and safe play area with a parent supervising.

  
How do I book a birthday party?
Call +8809666723423 with your date and the number of children. We'll explain the options.
