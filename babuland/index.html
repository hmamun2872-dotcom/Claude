Questions

    
Good to know

  

  
How do I book a birthday party?
Call 09666-723423 with your preferred date and the number of children. We will confirm availability and explain the options.

  
Is there a private area for the party?
Yes. Babuland has a private party area for cake cutting and celebration, next to the play zones.

  
Can we have food at the party?
Yes. Delicious food options are available. Tell us your guest count and preferences when you call.

  
What age is Babuland for?
It is made for children, with activities for different ages. Call us to ask about a specific age group.
